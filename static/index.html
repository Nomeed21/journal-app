✦ How this works
🧮
One XP ledger, no duplicate math
Every system — quests, achievements, boss battles, skill nodes — writes to a single xp_ledger table, idempotent per source. Level and totals are always read from it, never recomputed by summing tasks in five different places.
🌳
Mastery = 40% consistency + 60% completion
Each skill node's mastery % blends habit consistency (with slow decay on missed days, never an instant reset) and quest task completion — so showing up matters, but so does finishing.
🔥
Habits don't log themselves
There's no separate "log a habit" event. A habit's streak is derived from any completed Quest Board quest sharing its category — one source of truth for "did I actually do the thing today."
⚔️
The board writes itself
Quests auto-generate from Skills, Goals, Habits and journal entries. Skill-tree quests chain — completing a parent unlocks its child node's quest — and a recommendation engine scores everything by urgency, source, and difficulty to surface what matters most.
🔀
Specializations are a real choice
Some skill-tree branches fork into mutually exclusive paths — start one (e.g. AI over Backend or Cybersecurity) and its siblings lock immediately. There's no automatic un-commit; delete the goal it created if you want to switch.
🌐
Domains level up on their own — and pay you back
Each domain has its own level, separate from your overall one, and unlocks real perks as it climbs: bonus XP in that domain, extra Recovery Tokens for habits tagged with it, or its quests surfacing in Recommended more often. Check a domain's card to see what's active and what's next.
